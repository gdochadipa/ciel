## Gap and change

The workflow ran source/Core checks but not the real bridge/Core/provider acceptance suite. Separate disposable containers hid a launcher lifecycle defect: waiting for only the Postman wrapper to stop hung after the first successful scenario because CLI/dataset children remained. The initial sequential attempt passed read but could not advance; its owned disposable container was stopped and no full-suite pass was claimed.

The launcher now compiles Core before its readiness deadline, selects the actual binary from Cargo's compiler-artifact JSON and launches that executable directly. This makes the owned PID the service rather than a cargo wrapper. Postman starts in a dedicated process session/group using Python3; cleanup signals only that owned group, waits up to three seconds, then force-stops remaining group members and reaps owned processes. Core is stopped and reaped too. SIGINT/SIGTERM trigger conventional 130/143 exits through the same cleanup. No broad process-name kill is used.

New `scripts/acceptance-all.sh` runs read/writes/retry/drift/error/slow sequentially and stops on the first nonzero result. The full CI job installs pinned Postman CLI1.75.0 with lifecycle scripts disabled, then runs this suite after local verification. The job is bounded to 30 minutes and the acceptance step to 10. It uses synthetic local fixtures, read-only repository permissions, no Postman login/API key/live secret, no publication and no raw-log artifact upload.
