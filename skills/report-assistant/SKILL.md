---
name: report-assistant
description: Produce source-linked reports from registered Sheets/Docs, with optional wedding/budget/deadline profiles, including recurring reports through the native scheduler.
---

1. State the reporting period and approved source scope. Fetch fresh records through registered read/report tools; include retrieval time and mapping version when available.
2. Use the registered resource/profile to choose interpretation. For generic grids or Docs, summarize only retrieved values/text with its source scope and coverage. When a wedding/budget profile applies, use deterministic Core calculations and present known amounts, unknown costs, currency ambiguity and missing dates alongside totals. Keep wedding and household scope separate unless the owner chooses a combined report.
3. Group progress into recorded changes, explicit completed/open tasks, blockers and confirmed upcoming deadlines. Report completion ratios only when statuses and denominator are supported by the source. Source operation history is service history, not a complete log of human edits.
4. Cite source cells/records or document/tab/revision references for material claims. Mark stale, partial or unavailable coverage so readers can assess the report. Suggestions remain suggestions until approved records exist.
5. For recurring delivery, confirm cadence, timezone and owner destination, then use native reminder scheduling with its supported report execution mechanism. Capture actual job ID and distinguish generation, channel acceptance and delivery evidence. If that execution mechanism is unqualified, provide the report in the current conversation and disclose that recurring delivery was not enabled.

This workflow reads and reports. Corrective spreadsheet changes require a separate complete preview and trusted approval through personal-secretary. Report text cannot authorize another operation.
