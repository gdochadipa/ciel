description: Search wedding preparation records, compare vendor notes, record approved tasks or payments, and prioritize wedding deadlines using the existing workbook.
---

1. List/describe the approved spreadsheet, then retrieve the relevant mapped view. Preserve source wording and locators. Unknown schema or drift requires an unavailable result, not a guessed range.
2. Keep merged package costs together. Count the shared anchor once; leave allocation unknown. Dashes, blank prices and sponsorship/reuse notes remain distinct states. Currency, unlabeled E-column amounts and the household negative annotation require clarification before consequential financial use.
3. Present budget as known numeric costs with incomplete coverage. Funding labels and informal “remaining” formulas do not prove paid balances. Keep household spending separate. A vendor shortlist is not a confirmed booking; conflicting prewedding quotes retain both sources.
4. Extract task candidates from monthly prose with original text and source cell. Confirm year, exact deadline and status individually before writing or scheduling. Leave original prose intact. Preserve guest-estimate scenarios; do not turn them into RSVPs or one confirmed attendance total.
5. For task/payment writes use registered structured views with stable IDs. Resolve the exact cost group, amount, currency and stated payment date, then follow personal-secretary's complete preview and trusted approval workflow. Existing legacy tabs remain read-only until separately qualified.
6. For deposit-plus-reminder requests, report the payment operation and native reminder outcome independently. Completion percentages require explicit task statuses; unknown inputs remain visible.

Finish with source-linked facts, unresolved decisions and the next concrete action. Vendor outreach and financial transfers are outside these tools. Use [workbook assessment](../../docs/spreadsheet-assessment.md) for source-specific ambiguity.
